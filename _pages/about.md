Hi! I’m Clare Lohrmann, a computer scientist and researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human-robot interaction, and human-centered computing. I’m currently an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Western Washington University, where I teach and conduct research on how we can build intelligent systems that work more effectively with people. My work focuses particularly on human-robot teaming, predictability, and designing AI systems that are not only capable, but understandable and useful to the people who interact with them. Outside of research and teaching, I enjoy spending time on my hobby farm and finding ways to combine my love of technology, creativity, and the outdoors. Students interested in conducting research with me should send me an email to my WWU email address.
